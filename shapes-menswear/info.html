Rare Textiles & Care Guidance

160s 2-Ply Egyptian Giza Cotton

Hand-picked long staple cotton from the Nile delta. Spun into micro-fine yarns and two-ply twisted. Launder on delicate cold wash with mild eco-detergent; iron damp with medium-high steam.

Pure Normandy French Flax Linen

Harvested in coastal France, yielding an airy, naturally slubbed texture that regulates body temperature in humid tropical heat. Hand wash or gentle machine wash; line dry and embrace the natural relaxed linen crease.

Royal Indian Raw Mulberry Silk

Jewel-toned raw silks crafted for wedding cocktail receptions and regal celebrations. Strictly dry-clean only to protect hand-embroidered metallic zari threads.
